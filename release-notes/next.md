Changed

- Classic Chrome's trial tuning removes the added warm white-balance shift, colour-phase shift and negative exposure bias.

- Error logs are written in the background, with a separate crash record included in saved bug reports.
- Saving a recipe card releases its native buffer even if writing the picture fails.
- Updated Picture Profile recipes can be applied again even when their names have not changed.
- The list of keys on the About page and in the key log names the control wheel and the top dial again; they showed as blank gaps.
